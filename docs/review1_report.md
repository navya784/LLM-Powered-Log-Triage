The masking step yields 29 templates for 14 true event types; 12 of the 14 types are split into more than one template. Most of that comes from the minus sign of a negative block id being left outside the number mask (`blk_-<NUM>` vs `blk_<NUM>`): treating the two alike leaves 1 split type(s). The clustering step puts split pairs back together; where it merges different true types instead, they are listed under "Clusters that mix ground-truth classes" above. This is a known limitation (`docs/dataset_card.md`).

## 6. What this does and does not show

- Shows: one dataset, 2,000 real raw lines, turned into validated structured events and semantic groups, with every step configured, seeded and saved as files, and every group traceable to exact raw lines.
- Shows: how well events are grouped by log meaning *on this dataset*, measured against LogHub's template labels.
- **Does not show** that the system can identify root causes. HDFS_2k has no root-cause or anomaly labels, and good semantic grouping does not by itself demonstrate root-cause identification.
- Does not show that the method generalizes: this dataset has 14 near-deterministic event types, one service, no hosts and only INFO/WARN lines, and the grouping is nearly perfect with template embeddings. Harder datasets are needed before comparing methods.
